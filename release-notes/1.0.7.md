The editor you write plans, wrap-ups and Huddle posts in has been brought up to
date with the latest version of the component library TimeHuddle is built on.
The visible part is the formatting toolbar, which now behaves the way it looks.

## The toolbar keeps up with you

- **Heading and Lists apply what you pick.** Choose "Heading 2" or a bullet list
  from the toolbar and the text changes.
- **Buttons show what is on.** Bold, italic, alignment and the rest stay
  highlighted while that formatting applies to where your cursor is.
- **Highlight, superscript and subscript are kept.** They used to be dropped
  when a post was saved; now they come back when you open it again.

## Easier to read

- **Links in your own Huddle messages show up.** In light mode a link you posted
  was the same colour as your message bubble, so it looked like a gap in the
  sentence. It is now white and underlined, in light and dark.
- **Links inside the editor** use a colour that is readable on the dark background.
- **Code blocks** are coloured by language in both light and dark.

## Remove takes a member out of the organization

On the Organization Members page, **Remove** often left the person in the table,
or answered "Not an org member" about someone plainly listed.

- **Removing someone removes them.** They leave the organization and every team
  in it except their own Personal team, and they are not added back the next
  time they sign in.
- **Remove asks first** and says what it will do.
- **Anyone in the list can be removed**, including people who were only there
  because of a team.
- **A team is never left without an admin.** If the person is the only admin of
  a team, you are told which one, so you can give it another admin first.
- You can no longer remove yourself by accident; the button is off on your own row.

## Tickets fit any screen

The Tickets page is now one list of rows at every size, from a phone to a wide
monitor. The wide ten-column table that scrolled sideways on tablets and laptops
is gone.

- **The same row everywhere.** Each ticket shows where it sits (project,
  number, last update), then its title, then its badges under the title:
  assignees, status, priority, source and linked issue. A narrow screen only
  wraps them onto more lines. Nothing is hidden and nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Resizes as you go.** The list follows the space it has, so it adjusts
  smoothly as you resize the window or open and close the sidebar.
- **Sort and filter from the list's header.** Each property is a rounded
  button there. Issue #, Updated and Title sort when pressed, and reverse when
  pressed again. Assignees, Project, Source, Status and Priority open a menu
  of choices, with their sort at the top.
- **Pick several at once.** Assignees, Project, Status and Priority take more
  than one choice: each one you pick gets a tick, and a ticket matching any of
  them is listed. Assignees and Project have a search box for long lists.
- **A header that folds.** When the list is too narrow for every button, the
  ones that do not fit move behind a small arrow; press it to see them all.
- **Open and Closed at the top left.** The switch between open and closed
  tickets sits at the start of the list header and shows how many there are of
  each.
- **Timer first.** An open ticket starts with its timer button. A closed one
  shows a purple mark there instead.
- **My Board in the row menu.** A ticket's ⋮ menu now offers Add to My Board
  and Remove from My Board, as well as the button at the end of the row.
- **Select when you need it.** On a phone, press Select to show the checkboxes
  for bulk actions; Done clears the selection and hides them again. On a wide
  screen the checkboxes are always there.
