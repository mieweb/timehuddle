The editor you write plans, wrap-ups and Huddle posts in has been brought up to
date with the latest version of the component library TimeHuddle is built on.
The visible part is the formatting toolbar, which now behaves the way it looks.

## The toolbar keeps up with you

- **Heading and Lists apply what you pick.** Choose "Heading 2" or a bullet list
  from the toolbar and the text changes.
- **Buttons show what is on.** Bold, italic, alignment and the rest stay
  highlighted while that formatting applies to where your cursor is.
- **Highlight, superscript and subscript are kept.** They used to be dropped
  when a post was saved; now they come back when you open it again.

## Easier to read

- **Links in your own Huddle messages show up.** In light mode a link you posted
  was the same colour as your message bubble, so it looked like a gap in the
  sentence. It is now white and underlined, in light and dark.
- **Links inside the editor** use a colour that is readable on the dark background.
- **Code blocks** are coloured by language in both light and dark.

## Remove takes a member out of the organization

On the Organization Members page, **Remove** often left the person in the table,
or answered "Not an org member" about someone plainly listed.

- **Removing someone removes them.** They leave the organization and every team
  in it except their own Personal team, and they are not added back the next
  time they sign in.
- **Remove asks first** and says what it will do.
- **Anyone in the list can be removed**, including people who were only there
  because of a team.
- **A team is never left without an admin.** If the person is the only admin of
  a team, you are told which one, so you can give it another admin first.
- You can no longer remove yourself by accident; the button is off on your own row.

## Schedule a Huddle post for later

Write a post or reply now and choose when it goes out, so you don't interrupt
someone in the middle of their work.

- **Send later…** is on the arrow beside the send button. Pick a time — in an
  hour, tomorrow at 9:00, Monday at 9:00, or any date and time.
- **Only you see it until then.** At that time it appears for everyone and
  notifies them. Until it goes out, your post has a dashed outline and says
  when it is scheduled.
- **Scheduled (n)** lists your waiting posts, so you can send one now,
  reschedule it or delete it.
- **@mentions in Huddle posts now notify** the people you mention, on their
  phone too. A scheduled post notifies when it goes out.

## Create a team from anywhere

**+ New team** in the organization and team switcher now opens the Create Team
form right where you are, instead of sending you to the Teams page to find the
**+** button. The new team is created in the organization you have selected,
and it becomes your current team as soon as you create it.
