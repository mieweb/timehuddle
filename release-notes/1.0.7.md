The editor you write plans, wrap-ups and Huddle posts in has been brought up to
date with the latest version of the component library TimeHuddle is built on.
The visible part is the formatting toolbar, which now behaves the way it looks.

## The toolbar keeps up with you

- **Heading and Lists apply what you pick.** Choose "Heading 2" or a bullet list
  from the toolbar and the text changes.
- **Buttons show what is on.** Bold, italic, alignment and the rest stay
  highlighted while that formatting applies to where your cursor is.
- **Highlight, superscript and subscript are kept.** They used to be dropped
  when a post was saved; now they come back when you open it again.

## Easier to read

- **Links in your own Huddle messages show up.** In light mode a link you posted
  was the same colour as your message bubble, so it looked like a gap in the
  sentence. It is now white and underlined, in light and dark.
- **Links inside the editor** use a colour that is readable on the dark background.
- **Code blocks** are coloured by language in both light and dark.

## Videos, and links where you write

A ticket's, a Redmine issue's and a clock session's **Links and videos** are
now **Videos**, with two buttons: **Pulse** to record one on your phone, and
**Upload** for a file you already have. A clock session's videos open from the
🎥 button on its row in your timesheet.

A link — a YouTube Short, a Loom, any page — goes in the text you're already
writing: the ticket's description, your post, the reason for a timesheet
change. Links you attached before are still listed under **Videos**.

Links in text are now clickable wherever that text is shown: an approver
reading why you changed your timesheet, the note on a declined change (now
written under **Change declined** instead of hidden in a tooltip), a work
entry's note, a profile's bio, a team's description, and a Redmine issue's
history. The dashboard's recent posts read as plain sentences instead of
showing their formatting marks.

## Upload is back, beside Pulse

**Upload** sits beside **Pulse** on tickets, Redmine issues, clock sessions and
timesheet walkthroughs. Pick a video from your computer or phone and it goes to
the same place a Pulse video would, with the same message when it lands.

Any common video works: an iPhone `.mov`, a screen recording, a `.webm` from
your browser, an Android clip. TimeHuddle converts it to the same format the
Pulse app records, so it plays in every browser, and only adds it once it's
ready. The same goes for videos you add to a Huddle or Clock post, your
profile, or a timesheet change.

A file that isn't a video, or is larger than 500 MB, is turned away with a
message saying so — straight away, not after several tries.

## A walkthrough for a timesheet change, recorded or uploaded

When a change waits for approval — on the Work page, or an entry you added to
your timesheet — two buttons sit beside _Pending approval_: the Pulse logo to record a walkthrough on your phone, and
the upload arrow for a video you already have. The walkthrough is added to the
request as soon as it uploads, so your approver sees it alongside your
explanation. You can also attach one while you write the change.

## Pulse fits every screen

Every place Pulse appears was checked on a phone, a tablet, a laptop and a wide
screen. On the Work page the walkthrough moves under the entry instead of being
cut off. While a video uploads, its progress shows on the **Upload** button itself.
On the Clock page, the Pulse card fits whatever width it has. The Pulse window
fits a phone held sideways, and **Approve** is no longer cut short when
reviewing a change.

## Your timesheet, one session at a time

Your timesheet is a list of sessions now, not a wide table, so it reads the
same on a phone as on a laptop and nothing scrolls sideways. Each session shows
its day, its work time, and when you clocked in and out — in bold, with
**+1 day** when you clocked out after midnight. Two buttons open what's inside
it:

- **Breaks** — the session as it went, work and breaks in order, with the day
  marked where it runs past midnight.
- **Tickets** — the ticket timers you ran during it, each with its times.

A session waiting on a change says _Pending approval_ once, and a declined
change shows the reviewer's note once. An admin viewing someone's timesheet
from the Team tab sees the same list. On a phone the list, and the Work page's
entries, run edge to edge like Tickets.
