---
version: 1.0.6
date: 2026-10-05
title: A clock for every team
---

Each team now has its own clock. You can be on the clock in two teams at once,
and the Clock page, the header timer and the Break button always show the shift
of the team you're in — never another team's shift under this team's name.

## Clocked in somewhere else

When you're on the clock in another team, the Clock page says so: **Also on the
clock in Team A**, with a **Switch** button that takes you straight there. You
can still clock in to the team you're looking at.

## Every tab and device stays in step

Clock in or out on your laptop and your phone, or a second tab, catches up on
its own — whichever team each one has open. No more reloading to find out you
were already clocked in.

## Also in this release

- Clocking out of a team stops only the ticket timer running in that team's
  shift. A timer in another team's shift keeps going — and the same goes for
  the automatic clock-out after a long shift.
- Clocking in to a team twice — say from two tabs at once — no longer opens two
  shifts. The second attempt tells you that you're already clocked in.
- A ticket timer is recorded against the shift of the ticket's own team. If
  you're on the clock in more than one team and it isn't clear which shift a
  timer belongs to, you're asked to switch to that team first.
