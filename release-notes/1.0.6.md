Adding a video to a ticket, a Redmine issue or a clock session now starts from
a **Pulse** button that looks the same wherever it appears, and tells you where
your video went. And the first time you open **What's New** after an update has
landed, the page says so.

## Links and videos, side by side

A ticket's, a Redmine issue's and a clock session's attachments are now
**Links and videos**, with two buttons: **Link** for a pasted link — a YouTube
Short, a Loom, any page — and **Pulse** for a video of your own.

## Pulse: record on your phone, and it's there

Press **Pulse**. On a computer, a window shows a QR code to scan with your
phone; on a phone, it opens the Pulse app straight away — or the App Store /
Play Store if you don't have it yet. Record, upload, and the window says
**Added to this ticket** the moment the video lands, then closes on its own.
There's nothing to confirm or refresh.

If the ticket is deleted while you're recording, the video isn't lost: the
window tells you it was kept, and why.

Each Pulse link works for 30 minutes. Pressing **Pulse** again while a video is
still on its way reopens the same link, so nothing uploads twice.

## What's gone

**Upload from this device** on tickets. A video comes from Pulse.

## Confetti for a release you have not seen yet

TimeHuddle updates itself in the background, so a new version usually arrives
without you noticing. Open **What's New** after an update and you get a brief
burst of confetti — a signal that the release you are reading is new to this
device, not one you have already seen.

[Watch the confetti on a new release](https://youtube.com/shorts/CKS4XDvCxbI)

It fires once per version, per browser. Reading the page again, or opening it on
the same version tomorrow, is quiet. Open TimeHuddle on your phone after the
same update and you will get it there too: the celebration marks what the
install has caught up to, which is a different question from what you have
already read. The **New** flags on individual releases still follow your
account, so they stay consistent everywhere you sign in.

If you have **Reduce Motion** turned on in your system settings, the confetti is
skipped entirely and nothing else about the page changes.

## Huddle keeps your place and loads faster

Huddle no longer starts over every time you leave it. Open a conversation,
check a ticket, and come back: the same conversation is still open, with your
search and any half-written message, and nothing reloads.

- **Opens on Today.** Grouped by day, Huddle opens on today's conversation. If
  nobody has posted yet, you get an empty Today ready to write in. On a phone it
  goes straight into the chat instead of the list of days; the back arrow still
  takes you to the list.
- **Loads the last 30 days.** Older history is no longer fetched up front. Scroll
  to the end of the conversation list and the previous 30 days load, again and
  again, until there is nothing older ("No older posts").
- **No empty flash.** While posts load you see a spinner, not an empty Today,
  and the search and filters stay in place instead of jumping.

## Get the app from the App Store

TimeHuddle is on the App Store, so **Help → App Store** in the account menu now
opens the [TimeHuddle listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timehuddle/id6763657217)
instead of the old TestFlight beta invite. Install it from there, no beta
sign-up needed.

## Tickets fit any screen

The Tickets page is now one list of rows at every size, from a phone to a wide
monitor. The wide ten-column table that scrolled sideways on tablets and laptops
is gone.

- **Every detail on every row.** Each ticket shows its title, reference,
  project, last update, status, priority, source, linked issue and assignees. On
  a narrow screen they wrap onto more lines under the title; with more room they
  move up beside it. Nothing is hidden and nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Resizes as you go.** The list follows the space it has, so it adjusts
  smoothly as you resize the window or open and close the sidebar.
- **Sort and filter in one place.** Every sort and filter is in the menu at the
  end of the list header, in the same spot at every size.
- **Open and Closed at the top left.** The switch between open and closed
  tickets sits at the start of the list header and shows how many there are of
  each.
- **Select when you need it.** Press Select to show the checkboxes for bulk
  actions, at any screen size. Done clears the selection and hides them again.
