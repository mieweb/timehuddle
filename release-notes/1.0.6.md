Adding a video to a ticket or a Redmine issue now starts from a **Pulse** button
that looks the same wherever it appears, and tells you where your video went.

## Pulse, on tickets and Redmine issues

Under **Attachments**, press **Pulse**. On a computer, a window shows a QR code
to scan with your phone; it opens the Pulse app, or the App Store / Play Store
if you don't have it yet. Record, upload, and the window says **Added to this
ticket** the moment the video lands — then closes on its own. There's nothing to
confirm or refresh.

If the ticket is deleted while you're recording, the video isn't lost: the
window tells you it was kept, and why.

**Upload from this device** is still there, under the QR code, for a video file
you already have.

Each Pulse link works for 30 minutes. Pressing **Pulse** again while a video is
still on its way reopens the same link, so nothing uploads twice.
