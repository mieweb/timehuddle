TimeHuddle updates itself in the background, so a new version usually arrives
without you noticing. Now, the first time you open **What's New** after an
update has landed, the page says so.

## Confetti for a release you have not seen yet

Open **What's New** after an update and you get a brief burst of confetti — a
signal that the release you are reading is new to this device, not one you have
already seen.

[Watch the confetti on a new release](https://youtube.com/shorts/CKS4XDvCxbI)

It fires once per version, per browser. Reading the page again, or opening it on
the same version tomorrow, is quiet. Open TimeHuddle on your phone after the
same update and you will get it there too: the celebration marks what the
install has caught up to, which is a different question from what you have
already read. The **New** flags on individual releases still follow your
account, so they stay consistent everywhere you sign in.

If you have **Reduce Motion** turned on in your system settings, the confetti is
skipped entirely and nothing else about the page changes.

## Huddle keeps your place and loads faster

Huddle no longer starts over every time you leave it. Open a conversation,
check a ticket, and come back: the same conversation is still open, with your
search and any half-written message, and nothing reloads.

- **Opens on Today.** Grouped by day, Huddle opens on today's conversation. If
  nobody has posted yet, you get an empty Today ready to write in. On a phone it
  goes straight into the chat instead of the list of days; the back arrow still
  takes you to the list.
- **Loads the last 30 days.** Older history is no longer fetched up front. Scroll
  to the end of the conversation list and the previous 30 days load, again and
  again, until there is nothing older ("No older posts").
- **No empty flash.** While posts load you see a spinner, not an empty Today,
  and the search and filters stay in place instead of jumping.

## The rest of the app keeps your place too

Dashboard, Work, Teams, Activity Log and Organization now work the way Huddle
does: leave one and come back, and it is just as you left it, with nothing
reloading.

- **Same view.** The Dashboard comes back on the tab and view you had open
  (Team → Timesheet stays Team → Timesheet), and Work comes back on the day you
  were looking at.
- **Same scroll position**, instead of jumping back to the top.
- **Activity Log keeps what you loaded.** Events you pulled in with **Load
  more** stay, and anything new since your last visit is added on top.
- **Switching team or organization still starts fresh**, so you never see the
  last team's numbers under the new one's name.

When a page does have to load, you now see grey placeholders in the shape of
what is coming instead of a spinner on a blank page.

## Get the app from the App Store

TimeHuddle is on the App Store, so **Help → App Store** in the account menu now
opens the [TimeHuddle listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timehuddle/id6763657217)
instead of the old TestFlight beta invite. Install it from there, no beta
sign-up needed.
