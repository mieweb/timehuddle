A ticket for a Redmine issue now shows that issue as Redmine has it, and you can
work on it without leaving TimeHuddle.

## Redmine on the ticket page

When a ticket carries a Redmine issue, its page reads the issue with your own
Redmine account:

- **Status** is Redmine's, and you can only pick the moves Redmine allows you.
- **Title and description** are the issue's. Edit them in place and the change
  is saved to Redmine.
- **Priority** shows as Redmine has it.
- **Activity** includes the issue's comments, field changes and time logged in
  Redmine.
- **Changes made in Redmine show up on their own** when you come back to the
  tab, and every few minutes.

If someone changed the issue in Redmine while you were editing, TimeHuddle
says so instead of overwriting their change. If you can't see the issue in
Redmine, the ticket looks as it did before.

## A link is an issue

A ticket's link must now be an issue from one of your organization's sources:
a GitHub issue or pull request, or a Redmine issue. Other links, such as a
Google Doc or a Drive folder, go in the description, and TimeHuddle says so as
you type.

- **The link option is now called GitHub.** Paste a Redmine issue there and
  TimeHuddle offers to link it as a Redmine issue instead.
- **Links like that saved before** have been moved into their ticket's
  description, so nothing is lost.

## For organization owners

**Outside servers** in **Organization → Ticket IDs** now take each Redmine
server's full address, including any path it lives under. For now, members open
and edit issues on this TimeHuddle's own Redmine server; issues on other servers
show their ID and can be searched.

## Settings, one section at a time

Settings used to be one long column of cards. It now has a menu of sections —
Profile, Appearance, Notifications, API tokens, Integrations, and Account and
privacy — and shows one at a time. On a computer the menu sits down the side.
On a phone, Settings opens on the menu; pick a section to open it, and the
**‹** beside the Settings heading takes you back. It stays at the top while you
scroll.

- **Links go straight to a section.** "Connect Redmine" from a ticket opens
  Integrations, and coming back from GitHub opens Account and privacy.
- **Push notifications are a switch**, like Dark mode, in Notifications —
  next to **Send test notification**, which has moved there from the bottom of
  the sidebar.
- **Settings is in the sidebar** under Activity Log, as well as in your account menu.
- **The version and What's new** sit under the menu, in place of the old list
  of the tools the app is built with.
- **On a phone, Settings is lighter:** sections are plain lists instead of boxes
  inside the page.

## Long pages scroll to the end on phones

On a touchscreen, the last part of a long page could stay hidden behind the
bottom bar. Every page now scrolls all the way to its end.
