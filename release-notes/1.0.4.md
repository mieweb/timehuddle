version: 1.0.4
date: 2026-09-29
title: Huddle's feed is now an inbox
---

The Huddle feed has a new shape: instead of one long scroll of cards, or a single
chat thread for the whole team, posts and clock sessions are grouped into
conversations you can switch between — the way an inbox works.

## Group by

A **Group by** dropdown at the top of the conversation list groups your team's
posts. It starts on **Day** and remembers your choice:

- **Day** — everyone's posts from one calendar day.
- **Session** — one conversation per clock-in-to-clock-out, with your plan and
  wrap-up alongside the clock times. Posts made without clocking in get their
  own "Off the clock" conversation.
- **Person** — one conversation per teammate.
- **Ticket** — one conversation per ticket, plus a "No ticket" conversation for
  everything else.

Switching Group by is instant — it doesn't reload the feed, just re-groups what
is already there. Search, above it, looks through post text, people, tickets,
attachments and dates.

## Scope: your team, or everywhere you post

A **Team** dropdown beside Group by picks whose posts you see. Pick
**Personal** to see your own posts from the last 30 days, across every team
you're in, in one place, each labelled with its team.

## Posting

Post from the message box at the bottom of the conversation. Under the text box
are **Pulse** for recording or uploading a video, **Ticket** to link a ticket,
and **+** to attach files up to 25 MB. A Pulse video or a ticket can be posted
on its own, without any text. In **Personal**, a post goes to your Personal
team. Editing your own post in place works the same way it always has.

A team with no posts yet opens on a **Today** conversation with the message box
ready, so the first update can be written straight away.

On phones the inbox now fills the screen, and the message box sits right above
the bottom bar.

Your clock-in plan and clock-out wrap-up still have the full composer too, on
the Clock page.

## What's different

- **Comments are not shown right now.** Nothing is deleted — your comment
  history is safe — but the inbox doesn't have a place to show replies yet.
- **Likes are not shown right now**, for the same reason: nothing to click on in
  the inbox yet. Your past likes aren't gone, they're just not visible.
- **Drafts are gone.** The Huddle **Drafts** tab and the Clock page's **Save
  draft** button have been removed; write your plan and post it when you clock
  in.
- **Published posts can't be deleted right now**, and team admins can't edit or
  remove someone else's post. The inbox has no delete action yet.

Deep links to a specific post — from a notification, or Dashboard → Recent
Activity — still take you straight to it, now opening that post's session
conversation instead of scrolling to a card.
