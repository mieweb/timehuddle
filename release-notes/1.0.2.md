title: The app updates itself
---

TimeHuddle on iOS and Android no longer waits on an App Store review to pick up
a fix. Updates download in the background and apply the next time you open the
app.

## Always on the current version

When an update is waiting, the app fetches it and switches over on its own. If a
release fixes something that genuinely cannot be left running — a bad
calculation, a security fix — the app holds at an update screen until the new
version has downloaded, rather than letting you keep working against it.

You can check which version you are on from the **More** sheet on mobile, or
beside the organisation switcher on the web.
