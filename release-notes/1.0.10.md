On the mobile app, going back now works the way your phone does. Swipe or press back to return to the page you were just on.

## Going back

- **iPhone and iPad:** swipe from the left edge of the screen to go back to the previous page.
- **Android:** the system back gesture or button goes back to the previous page. On the first page it sends the app to the background instead of closing it.

The page you return to shows exactly what you left, including the selected menu item and any filters in the address.
