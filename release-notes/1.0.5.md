Every video in TimeHuddle now comes from the Pulse app. Record it, upload it,
and it goes straight where you started it: a Huddle post, your plan or
wrap-up, a ticket, a clock session, or a timesheet change.

A Pulse video is never lost. If it can't go where you recorded it for (say,
the change was approved while you were recording), it's kept, and the Pulse
window tells you why.

## Adding a video: record it, and it's done

Every button that opens Pulse looks the same: the Pulse logo and what it
does. Press it, record in the Pulse app, upload — and the video goes straight
where you started it. There's nothing to attach or post afterwards. The name
you give your draft in Pulse becomes the video's title.

- **Clock page:** a Pulse section under the clock lets you clock in or out
  with a video. **Clock in with Pulse** posts the video to Huddle as your plan
  and clocks you in. **Clock out with Pulse** adds it as your wrap-up and
  clocks you out. It's there whether or not your team requires a plan. While
  that video is on its way, the plan or wrap-up post button waits for it, so
  you don't post twice.
- **Huddle:** **Post with Pulse**, beside **Share an update**, posts the video
  to the team as its own post.
- **Tickets and clock sessions:** **Add video with Pulse** in **Links and
  videos** adds the video there.
- **Timesheet changes that need approval:** after you send the change, **Add
  walkthrough with Pulse** beside **Pending approval** adds a video walkthrough
  for the approver.

On a computer, Pulse shows a QR code to scan with your phone, and tells you
when the video has landed. If you leave the page before it lands, a message
still tells you where it went. On a phone it opens the Pulse app, or the App Store
/ Play Store if you don't have it yet. Each Pulse link works for 30 minutes.

To share a video from somewhere else, like a YouTube Short or a Loom, paste its
link into your post. On a ticket or clock session, use the **Link** button next
to **Add video with Pulse** in **Links and videos**: paste the link, and it's
added.

## What's gone

Picking a video file from your computer or phone is no longer possible. These
were removed:

- The **Video** button in Huddle's composer and in the Clock-page composer.
  Dropping or pasting a video file into a post now tells you to use Pulse
  instead.
- **Upload from this device** in the Pulse window, in Huddle and on tickets.
- The **Feed** tab on your profile, with its video uploads. It's being
  redesigned, and profiles now open on **Work**.
- Attaching a video file to a timesheet change that needs approval. The
  walkthrough is now added with Pulse after you send the change, and it's
  optional: adding past time no longer requires a video.
- **Pulse** in the post composer's button row. It now sits beside
  **Share an update** (and in the Clock page's Pulse section), because a Pulse
  video posts on its own.

Videos you've already posted stay where they are and keep playing.
