title: Your Redmine issues, inside TimeHuddle
---

If your team tracks work in Redmine, you no longer have to keep it in a second
tab. Connect your Redmine account once and your issues show up on the Tickets
page next to your TimeHuddle tickets — searchable, editable, and ready to clock
time against.

## Connecting your account

Go to **Settings → Redmine** and paste your personal Redmine API key. Everything
TimeHuddle does from then on runs under your own key, so Redmine applies the
permissions and workflow you already have, and anything you create or change is
recorded as you — not as a shared robot account.

## Finding an issue

Your Redmine issues appear in the Tickets table alongside internal tickets, and
the source column tells you which is which.

The search bar above the table does double duty. It filters the rows you can
already see, and it opens a dropdown of Redmine issues you may be looking for:

- **Suggested for you** — issues you're assigned to or recently touched, shown
  as soon as you click the box, narrowed as you type.
- **More from Redmine** — a live search of your Redmine instance when what you
  want isn't already in the list.

Search by issue number, subject, assignee, or paste a Redmine link.

Suggestions you don't want are dismissible — press Delete on a highlighted row
and it stays hidden for 15 days. **Settings → Redmine → Hidden suggestions**
lists everything you've dismissed, with a **Restore** button.

Searches are held in memory for the session only. Nothing you type in that box
is written to your browser's storage or to the page URL.

## Working on an issue

Open any Redmine issue to get a full detail page: description, status,
assignee, priority, the issue's activity history, the attachments on it, and
your own time entries against it.

From the Tickets table you can also:

- **Create an issue** — **New Ticket → Redmine issue**, then pick a project,
  tracker, subject, description, assignee and priority.
- **Edit one** — the row's **⋮** menu has **Edit Ticket** and **Change Status**.
  The status list only offers the transitions Redmine actually allows you, and
  only the fields you changed get sent. If someone else edited the issue while
  your dialog was open, the save is refused rather than overwriting their work,
  and you're told to reopen it.

Issue deletion, tags and custom fields aren't supported from TimeHuddle — those
still belong in Redmine itself.

## Clocking time

Start a timer straight from a Redmine issue, the same way you would on a
TimeHuddle ticket. Starting one pins the issue so it stays on your Tickets list
and My Board for as long as you're working on it.

Pins are capped at 500 per person. At the cap the timer still starts and still
records your time — the issue just doesn't get added to My Board, and a message
tells you so rather than failing silently.

## Also in this release

- Tickets that pointed at a board column that no longer existed were stranded
  and invisible on My Board. They're back where they belong.
- The account menu on phones now has the same items as the desktop one.
- Clocking in while starting work on a ticket is more reliable.

## Still to come

The Huddle inbox introduced in 1.0.4 is unchanged here, including its gaps —
comments, likes, drafts and deleting a published post are still unavailable.
They haven't been forgotten; they need work in the underlying inbox component
before they can come back.
