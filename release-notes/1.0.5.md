title: Videos come from Pulse, and land where you started them

Every video in TimeHuddle now comes from the Pulse app. Record it, upload it,
and it goes straight where you started it: a Huddle post, your plan or
wrap-up, a ticket, a clock session, or a timesheet change.

## Your media library

The **Feed** tab on your profile is now your **Media library**: every image,
document and Pulse video you've added in Huddle posts, on tickets and on your
clock sessions. Teammates who open your profile see the videos on tickets and
sessions from teams they're in. Timesheet walkthroughs stay between you and
your approver.

A Pulse video is never lost. If it can't go where you recorded it for (say,
the change was approved while you were recording), it's kept in your Media
library and the Pulse window tells you why.

## Adding a video: record it, and it's done

Press **Pulse**, record in the Pulse app, upload — and the video goes straight
where you started it. There's nothing to attach or post afterwards:

- **Huddle:** the **Pulse** button beside **Share an update** posts the video
  to the team as its own post, titled with your Pulse draft's name.
- **Clock page:** the **Pulse** button beside **Post plan and clock in** makes
  the video your plan and clocks you in. At the end of the day, the one beside
  **Post wrap-up and clock out** adds the video as your wrap-up and clocks you
  out. While that video is on its way, the post button waits for it, so you
  don't post your plan or wrap-up twice.
- **Tickets and clock sessions:** **Pulse** in **Links and videos** adds the
  video there.
- **Timesheet changes that need approval:** after you send the change, **Pulse**
  beside **Pending approval** adds a video walkthrough for the approver.

On a computer, Pulse shows a QR code to scan with your phone, and tells you
when the video has landed. On a phone it opens the Pulse app, or the App Store
/ Play Store if you don't have it yet. Each Pulse link works for 30 minutes.

To share a video from somewhere else, like a YouTube Short or a Loom, paste its
link into your post. On a ticket or clock session, use the **Link** button next
to **Pulse** in **Links and videos**: paste the link, and it's added.

## What's gone

Picking a video file from your computer or phone is no longer possible. These
were removed:

- The **Video** button in Huddle's composer and in the Clock-page composer.
  Dropping or pasting a video file into a post now tells you to use Pulse
  instead.
- **Upload from this device** in the Pulse window, in Huddle and on tickets.
- Video uploads on your profile page. You can still upload images there, and
  your Pulse videos still show there.
- Attaching a video file to a timesheet change that needs approval. The
  walkthrough is now added with Pulse after you send the change, and it's
  optional: adding past time no longer requires a video.
- **Pulse** in the post composer's button row. It now sits beside
  **Share an update** (and beside the Clock page's post buttons), because a
  Pulse video posts on its own.

Videos you've already posted stay where they are and keep playing.
