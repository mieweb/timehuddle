## Report a problem

Inside the app, open the **Help** menu and choose **Report an Issue**. Or open an issue directly on [GitHub](https://github.com/mieweb/timehuddle/issues) — say what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

## Share an idea

**Help → Share Your Feedback** in the app sends a suggestion straight to the team. Ideas are welcome as GitHub issues too.

## What changed?

Every release is described in plain language in the [release notes](/release-notes). Signed in, you will see a **What's new** badge when there is something you haven't read.

## Common questions

**I forgot my password.** Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen; a reset link is emailed to you.

**My timer won't start.** A ticket timer needs an open shift. Clock in first — TimeHuddle offers to do it for you.

**I can't clock out.** If your team requires a plan, add a wrap-up to the session's post first.

**Why don't my shift and ticket totals match?** They measure different things — see [Tickets and timers](/docs/tickets-and-timers#shift-time-and-ticket-time-differ).

**My Redmine hours aren't in Redmine.** Hours are only sent when you press **Send work entries to Redmine**, after clocking out. See [Redmine](/docs/redmine#send-your-hours).
