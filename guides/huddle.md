The **Huddle** is where a team's day is written down. Much of it writes itself: the plan you post when you clock in, the wrap-up when you clock out, and a one-line update each time you start, switch or stop a ticket timer.

## Read it your way

Group the feed by **day**, by **session**, by **person** or by **ticket**. Switch between the current team and **Personal**, which gathers your own posts from the last 30 days across every team you are in.

Search covers text, people, tickets, attachments and dates.

## Write a post

Posts are markdown — headings, lists, code, tables and even Mermaid diagrams render as you would expect.

- Type **@** to mention a teammate.
- Link a post to a **ticket** so it shows up with that ticket's work.
- Paste or drag in **screenshots and documents** — up to 25 MB each.

## Video with Pulse

[Pulse Cam](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6748621024) is MIE's companion video app. On a computer, TimeHuddle shows a QR code; scan it with your phone, record, and the video lands where you started — a Huddle post, a ticket, a clock-in plan or wrap-up, or the evidence for a timesheet change request.
