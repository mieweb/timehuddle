## Where timesheets are

- **Your own:** Dashboard → **Me** → Timesheet. Each shift is listed with the ticket sessions nested under it.
- **Your team's:** Dashboard → **Team**, for team admins, beside the queue of change requests.

## Asking for a change

Recorded time is not edited quietly. To **edit**, **delete** or **add** a shift or a ticket-timer entry, you send a request:

1. Choose the entry (or **add** a missing one) and make the change.
2. Explain why — at least ten characters.
3. For a brand-new entry, attach a short video recorded with Pulse as evidence.

Each entry can have one pending request at a time, and you can cancel it while it waits.

## Reviewing a request

Requests go to the team's admins — never to the person who asked.

- **Approve** applies the change.
- **Decline** needs a note telling them why.
- If the entry changed while the request was waiting, approval is refused, so nobody approves a change to time that no longer looks the way it did.

The requester is notified of the decision.

## When no approval is needed

- In your **personal workspace**.
- When you are the **only admin** of a team — your changes apply directly, since there is no one else to approve them.
