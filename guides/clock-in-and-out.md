A **shift** is your working time for one team, from clock-in to clock-out, minus meal breaks. It is separate from the ticket timers you run during it — see [Tickets and timers](/docs/tickets-and-timers) for those.

## Clock in

Open **Clock** and press the clock-in button. The header link and the centre button of the mobile bottom bar both take you there.

- **Plans.** If your team requires a plan, you write it first — what you intend to work on — and it is posted to the team's Huddle as you clock in. New teams require a plan by default; an admin can turn it off.
- **Who sees it.** Your team's admins get a notification that you clocked in.

While you are clocked in, the header shows a live timer and a **Break** button on every page.

## Take a break

Press **Break** to pause the shift, and **Resume** to carry on.

- Starting a break **stops your running ticket timer**, and resuming starts it again as a new session — so break time never lands on a ticket.
- A break of **20 minutes or more** counts as a meal break and comes off your shift time. Shorter breaks count as worked time.

## Clock out

Clock out from the Clock page.

- If your team requires a plan, you add a **wrap-up** to the same post first — what actually got done.
- Clocking out **stops any ticket timer** still running and closes any open break.
- You and your team's admins are notified with the shift's length.

## Reminders and the 8-hour safety net

TimeHuddle watches for clocks left running:

| When                      | What happens                                    |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 4 hours after clocking in | A reminder to take a break                      |
| 7 h 45 min of work        | A reminder that you are approaching eight hours |
| 8 hours of work           | You are clocked out automatically, and told so  |

Work time here excludes meal breaks, the same as the shift clock.

## Your timesheet

Every shift appears in **Dashboard → Me → Timesheet**, with the ticket sessions you ran nested under the shift they happened in. Got a time wrong? You can ask to change it — see [Timesheets and approvals](/docs/timesheets).
