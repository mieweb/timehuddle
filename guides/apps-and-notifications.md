## On your phone

The iPhone app is on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timehuddle/id6763657217). It is the same TimeHuddle as the web — same account, same data, same features — and an Android app is built from the same code.

Most updates arrive **over the air**: the app downloads them in the background and applies them the next time it starts, without waiting on store review.

## Notifications

Notifications collect under **Notifications** in the app and, once you allow it, arrive as push notifications — in the browser, and natively on iPhone and Android. **Settings** has a push section with a test button.

You will hear about:

| Area       | Notifications                                                               |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your shift | the 4-hour break reminder, the approaching-8-hours reminder, auto clock-out |
| Tickets    | being assigned a ticket, a ticket's link changing                           |
| Timesheets | a change to your entries, a request to review, the decision on yours        |
| Teams      | join requests (admins), your request approved or declined, setting changes  |
| Admins     | teammates clocking in and out                                               |
| The Huddle | comments on your posts                                                      |
