## Teams

A **team** is who you clock in for. Create one and you become its **admin**; everyone else joins as a **member**.

### Letting people in

| Way in            | What happens                                          |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Join code (typed) | A request waits for an admin to approve or decline it |
| Link or QR code   | The person joins straight away                        |
| Email invitation  | Joins when accepted; the invitation lasts seven days  |

Turn on **auto-accept** if you want typed codes to join without approval.

### Roles

- **Admins** approve join requests, change roles, remove members, review timesheet changes and see the team's timesheet.
- The **last admin** of a team cannot be demoted, so a team is never left without one.
- Teams can have **sub-teams**.

### What admins see

The **Dashboard → Team** view shows who is online and clocked in, the timers running, and the team's timesheet. Admins are notified when someone clocks in or out.

## Organizations and the enterprise

Above teams sit **organizations**, and above those an **enterprise** — the whole installation.

- **Organizations** have owners, admins and members. They hold teams, invitations and a **reports-to** structure drawn as an org chart. Owners and admins also get a **Usage** page showing activity over the last 7, 14 or 30 days.
- **The enterprise** is claimed by the first person to set up the installation. Its owners and admins manage organizations and installation-wide defaults.

## Settings that cascade

A setting chosen at the enterprise or organization level is **inherited** by every team below it, and can be **locked** so teams cannot override it. A team's own settings — like whether clocking in requires a plan — apply to that team alone.
