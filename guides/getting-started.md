TimeHuddle tracks two things: the **shift** you clock in and out of for a team, and the **ticket timers** you run on individual pieces of work during it. Everything else — the team feed, timesheets, Redmine — is built on those two records.

## 1. Make an account

Open TimeHuddle and choose **Sign up**. You can use an email and password, or sign in with one of the providers your installation offers (GitHub, Google or Apple). If you forget your password, **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen emails you a reset link.

The first time you sign in you will be asked to claim a **username** — it is how teammates @mention you.

On iPhone, you can also install [TimeHuddle from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timehuddle/id6763657217). It is the same account and the same data.

## 2. Join your team

Every account starts with a **personal workspace** for your own notes and tickets. Real work happens in a **team**. There are three ways in:

- **A join code** — type the code your admin gives you. A team admin approves the request (some teams accept automatically).
- **A link or QR code** — opening an invite link joins you straight away.
- **An email invitation** — accept it within seven days.

Starting something new? Create a team yourself and you become its admin. See [Teams and organizations](/docs/teams).

## 3. Clock in

Open **Clock** and press the clock-in button. If your team asks for a plan first, write a line or two about what you intend to do — it is posted to the team's Huddle.

While you are clocked in, the header shows your running time and a **Break** button. Read [Clock in and out](/docs/clock-in-and-out) for breaks, reminders and the 8-hour safety net.

## 4. Time your work

Open **Tickets**, pick a ticket — or create one with **New Ticket** — and start its timer. One timer runs at a time; starting another stops the first. See [Tickets and timers](/docs/tickets-and-timers).

## 5. Clock out

When you are done, clock out from the Clock page. Any running timer stops with you. Your day now appears in your timesheet under **Dashboard → Me**.

## Find your way around

- **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** opens the command palette: jump to any page, find a ticket or a teammate, or switch between light and dark.
- **What's new** — every release is described in the [release notes](/release-notes).
